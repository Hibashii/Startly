Startly: setting the hook

Hook only, with the highlighter on "Start here.", as you chose. The font is still SF Pro. Only its size, weight and spacing change between A–D, so you can see what made your eyes get lost.

  • A · Big & bold: what you saw before. Both lines are huge and heavy, so nothing leads your eye.
  • B · Smaller, lighter: same idea, about 20% smaller and less heavy.
  • C · Two tones (my pick): the question is softer and gray, and "Start here." is strong with the highlighter. You read the question in passing and land on the answer.
  • D · Small question, big answer: the question becomes a small line on top and "Start here." is the big headline. The strongest contrast.

Colors: A is the current set. B is a softer, more refined set (Iris, Ocean, Sage, Plum, Coral, Ink). Open 🎨 in the app to see each one.
