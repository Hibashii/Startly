What Startly is, in one breath

You tell Startly about your day (or week) in plain words. It turns that into a real timetable, keeps it on time when the day goes off track, and cheers you on as you check things off. There are four main pieces:

  • The screen (src/components/): the timetable, week view, chat, and timer.
  • The store (src/state/store.ts): everything you see, saved in your browser (localStorage). There's no database and no account.
  • The assistant (server/): one small route that asks the OpenAI model what you meant and gets back structured actions.
  • The scheduler (src/scheduler/): plain code that turns those actions into times, with lunch, breaks, fixed events and your end of day.

One action, end to end: “dump your day”

A reference route (3 stops)

Open these in your editor and search for the anchor in bold.

1. Your message leaves the screen

src/state/store.ts, search send: async. It adds your message to the chat, catches a typed “undo” in code, then builds what the AI is allowed to see with buildContext in src/lib/api.ts (today's timetable, tasks, settings, recent chat) and calls askStartly.

2. The AI says what you meant, not when things happen

server/app.ts, search app.post('/api/assistant', which calls callAssistant in server/assistant.ts. The rules Startly follows live in server/prompt.ts. The answer must match the shapes in shared/schema.ts (a reply, optional questions, and a list of actions like add task, move to day, or set end of day). Both the browser and the server use that file, so they can't disagree.

3. Code places it on the timetable

src/scheduler/applyActions.ts, search export function applyActions. Invalid actions are skipped. Today is laid out by reshuffleToday in src/scheduler/schedule.ts and later days by planDay in src/scheduler/days.ts. The hard limits are enforced here: fixed events don't move, there's lunch and breaks, and nothing goes past your end of day. src/components/timetable/Timetable.tsx shows the result.

To change this, start here

  • How Startly thinks or talks to the AI: server/prompt.ts. Then run npx tsx scripts/real-check.ts more than once.
  • A new thing the AI can do: add the action to shared/schema.ts, then handle it in src/scheduler/applyActions.ts.
  • Lunch, breaks, end-of-day rules: src/scheduler/schedule.ts (tests in schedule.test.ts).
  • Any words on screen, in all 6 languages: src/i18n/en.ts and its siblings (fr, ar, es, de, nl). Which cheer or greeting is chosen: src/lib/messages.ts.
  • Accent colors and dark mode: src/index.css, search data-accent.
  • Phone layout: src/components/MainScreen.tsx and TopBar.tsx; covered by e2e/mobile.spec.ts.

A practice to reuse

Turn a real conversation that went wrong into a check you can replay against the real model.

Mocked tests (npm test, npm run e2e) prove the code does what it should with a given AI answer. They can't prove the AI gives a good answer. On Startly, the bugs that mattered showed up in real use. For example, planning a messy week asked 10+ questions and then forgot the tasks from the first message.

How that one was found, fixed, and locked in
  1. Cause: the AI only saw the last 10 messages, so after enough questions the original request had scrolled out of view.
  2. Fix in code, not just the prompt: conversation() in src/lib/api.ts now sends everything since the request while Startly is still asking, plus a questionsAsked count. The rule in server/prompt.ts says to stop asking at 2.
  3. Evidence: the scenario “Planning a vacation week” in scripts/real-check.ts replays that conversation against the real model and checks the fixed events, Sunday off, the start time, and no duplicates. Run it with npx tsx scripts/real-check.ts week.
  4. Why more than once: the model's answers vary between runs, so one green run proves little. A few green runs in a row is real evidence.

On your next AI feature, write down the exact thing a user typed and the result you expected before you fix it. Then make it a replayable scenario. It becomes your proof that the fix works, and your alarm if a prompt change breaks it later.
